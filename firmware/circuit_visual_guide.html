Hardware Assembly Blueprint

SafeGuard GPS Safety Wearable — Visual Circuit Diagram

ESP32-C3 Super Mini + SIM800L GSM + GY-NEO6MV2 GPS + TTP223B Touch + Electret Mic + Mini Speaker

⚠️
Critical Assembly Warnings:

1. Capacitors Polarity: The white stripe with '-' markings MUST connect to GND. Long leg is POSITIVE (+).

2. SIM800L RX Voltage Divider: Do NOT connect ESP32 GPIO5 directly to SIM800L RX. Use the 1kΩ + 330Ω divider to step 3.3V down to ~0.82V.

3. Power Surge: SIM800L peaks up to 2A during transmission. Keep the 2x 470µF capacitors as close as possible to the SIM800L VCC/GND pins.

🛠️ Step-by-Step Breadboard Order

  1. Straddle ESP32-C3: Place across the breadboard centre ravine.
  2. Power Rails: Connect ESP32 3V3 to + rail, and GND to − rail.
  3. SIM800L Power & Caps: Connect 2× 470µF capacitors across VCC & GND right beside the module. Watch capacitor negative stripe!
  4. Voltage Divider: GPIO5 → 1kΩ → [Node] → 330Ω → GND. Connect SIM800L RX to [Node].
  5. SIM800L TX: Wire directly to GPIO4.
  6. GPS Module: VCC to 3V3 rail, GND to rail, TX to GPIO6, RX to GPIO7.
  7. Touch Module: VCC to 3V3, GND to rail, SIG to GPIO2.
  8. Mic Capsule: + pin to GPIO1, 10kΩ from + pin to 3V3 rail, − pin to GND.
  9. Speaker: GPIO8 → 22Ω → SPK+, SPK− to GND.
